## [0.1.0] — 2026-11-06

Initial documented Phase 1 baseline for the contributor portal.

### Added

#### Auth & enrollment
- CareMate Patient ID → email OTP verification (OTP never returned to the browser)
- Join admin-created active chapters (no profile duplication)

#### Chapter experience
- Chapter hub with events, announcements, resources, and gallery
- Contribution points with chapter and national leaderboards
- Badges and certificates (admin-awarded)
- In-app notifications

#### Premium
- CareMate Premium subscribe handoff to the payment host (Paystack NGN / USD)
